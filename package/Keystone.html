Keystone

Rhino plugin · v1.10.0 · updated 10/10/2026

New

Keystone by Seraphin Ernst – accurate Rhino 8 tools for architects. VectorView: vector drawings with exact shadows from the view you work in – isometric, elevation, plan or perspective, including two-point perspective. The isometric is drawn true to length (DIN), so it measures correctly at scale; elevations and plans are true to scale; shadows are computed from Rhino's sun. Every visible face and every cast shadow becomes a closed, non-overlapping region with a solid hatch and its outline, exported to DXF (shadows truly transparent) or DWG at 1:1 in millimetres – shadows above the faces, optionally one layer per component. Commands: VectorView, VectorViewSettings, VectorViewPanel. TrussBuilder: trusses sized while you design. Plane trusses along any curve (Pratt, Howe, Warren, K, X) and steel space frames over a rectangle, with Eurocode-based preliminary design – steel EN 1993, timber EN 1995 – showing utilisation, deflection, mass and quantities in a live preview of the sized result. Preliminary design only; it does not replace structural verification by an engineer. Commands: TrussBuilder, TrussBuilderSpaceFrame, TrussBuilderPanel. BrickBuilder: brick walls laid as on site. Walls along any curve in real bonds – stretcher, raking stretcher, header, English, English cross, Flemish, Monk, Silesian, American, wild and stack bond – in NF, DF, 2DF, Waal, UK, US or custom formats, from half a brick up to four bricks thick with the bond carried through the whole wall. Corners and closers follow masonry rules; with whole bricks the head joints adjust to the wall length instead of cutting bricks. Windows and doors go into finished walls – with soldier courses, flat, segmental or round arches, precast or concrete lintels and rowlock sills. Course plans and a pieces list with order quantities. Commands: BrickBuilder, BrickBuilderOpening, BrickBuilderPanel. StairBuilder: stairs that pass the code. Draw the walking line, enter the storey height – straight, L and U stairs with landings, winder stairs, spiral and helical stairs, checked against DIN 18065 (also ASR A1.8, DIN 18040, OIB-RL 4, SIA 358, Approved Document K, IRC/IBC) with the calculation shown, built in steel, timber, reinforced concrete or stone with a preliminary design, or as plain geometry – also over several storeys, with floor landings at the exits, and in any width. Preliminary design only; it does not replace structural verification by an engineer. Commands: StairBuilder, StairBuilderEdit, StairBuilderPanel. All tools share one docked Keystone panel (command KeystonePanel).

How to install Keystone

  1. Click Install in Rhino to open Rhino's Package Manager with Keystone selected, or run _PackageManager inside Rhino and search for Keystone.On the computer running Rhino, run _PackageManager and search for Keystone.
  2. Or install it from a terminal (assumes a default Rhino 8 installation):
    Windows (PowerShell)
    & "C:\Program Files\Rhino 8\System\Yak.exe" install Keystone
    macOS (Terminal)
    "/Applications/Rhino 8.app/Contents/Resources/bin/yak" install Keystone
  3. Or download a .yak file and drag it onto an open Rhino window.
    • Keystone-1.10.0-rh8_0-any.yakWindows & Mac · Rhino 8.0

Restart Rhino once the install finishes.
